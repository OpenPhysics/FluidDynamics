# Credits — Fluid Dynamics

Interactive simulation of flow past an obstacle: laminar streamlines, the Kármán
vortex street, and the transition to a turbulent wake. The solver is Jos Stam's
*Stable Fluids*, running in WGSL compute shaders on WebGPU.

## Method

- Stam, J. (1999). *Stable Fluids.* SIGGRAPH '99, 121–128.
- Fedkiw, R., Stam, J. & Jensen, H. W. (2001). *Visual Simulation of Smoke.*
  SIGGRAPH '01, 15–22. (Vorticity confinement.)
- Harris, M. (2004). *Fast Fluid Dynamics Simulation on the GPU.* GPU Gems, ch. 38.
